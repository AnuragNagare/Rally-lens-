Court Sense

For three days my ball tracker followed a man’s hat. It was small, bright and roughly the right colour, so the system tracked it around the court with total confidence.

I wanted to know how much a single fixed camera can tell you about a pickleball match. No sensors, no second angle.

Give Court Sense ordinary match footage and it returns:
• Every shot, and who hit it
• Average speed of each shot
• Distance each player ran
• Time each held the kitchen line

In a 20-second clip it found all 16 shots, with none invented.

What’s underneath:
• Four court corners turn pixels into metres
• Pose estimation places each player by their feet
• A stricter ball colour test cut false detections from five per frame to under one (goodbye, hat)
• Shots are counted where the ball turns around between players, so bounces don’t count

One clip so far, so it proves the method, not the accuracy.
